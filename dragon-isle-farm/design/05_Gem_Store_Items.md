# 05 — Gem Store Items

**Covers:** gear, upgrades and cosmetics sold in the Gem Store · **Rules from:** `01_Art_Direction.md`

Prices and effects are unchanged from `GameConfig`. Gear has a visible object on the player's farm so other players can see what they own.

---

## Golden Watering Can — Gear, 300 Gems
- **Effect:** +10% grow speed (permanent).
- **Look:** chunky gold can (`Foil`, `#FFBE28`) with the spout carved as a small dragon head; rune ᛃ (Jera, "harvest") engraved on the side.
- **On the farm:** hangs on a post by the farm gate.
- **In use:** whenever the owner plants a seed, a short sparkling water arc pours onto the tile.
- **Icon:** the can at three-quarter view, water drops falling from the dragon's mouth.

## Rune Totem — Gear, 500 Gems
- **Effect:** +2% mutation chance (permanent).
- **Look:** a 6-stud carved slate pillar `#6E6E73` with three stacked runes glowing cyan `#3CE6F0`, topped with a small dragon skull carving in pale stone.
- **On the farm:** stands at the back corner of the soil grid.
- **In use:** the runes pulse whenever a crop on the farm mutates, and a ring of light briefly shows over that tile.
- **Icon:** the pillar with runes glowing.

## Extra Pet Slot — Upgrade, 400 Gems (max 5 slots)
- **Effect:** +1 equipped pet.
- **Look:** UI only. A paw print inside a rune circle.
- **Pets window:** empty slots show as dashed rune circles; a newly bought slot glows once.

## Viking Banner — Cosmetic, 100 Gems
- **Look:** a 14-stud wooden pole with a carved dragon-head finial; red cloth `#B41E28` with cream trim `#F2E6C8` and a crossed-wheat-and-dragon-wing emblem.
- **Motion:** cloth made of 3 hinged segments that sway in a loop (cheap fake wind).
- **Placement:** at the farm gate (BannerSpot, already in the prototype).
- **Later:** let players pick the banner colour (another Gem sink).

## Dragon Statue — Cosmetic, 750 Gems
- **Look:** a sitting dragon carved in grey stone `#96969B`, wings folded, on a mossy plinth (`#50823A` moss patches). An original pose and design, not modelled on any existing dragon.
- **Motion:** eyes glow amber `#FFB04A` at night.
- **Placement:** StatueSpot (already in the prototype).
- **Prototype change:** currently a scaled grey Thunder Dragon pet; replace it with this statue.
